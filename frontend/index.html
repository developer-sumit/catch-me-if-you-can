About the Project

SoulServe is an intelligent food waste redistribution platform designed to bridge the gap between food abundance and food scarcity. By leveraging AI forecasting and real-time geolocation, we empower Food Providers to effortlessly donate surplus meals to local Receivers, ensuring that good food nourishes communities instead of ending up in landfills.

How It Works

Three simple steps to turn food waste into meals for those in need.

📝
1

Log Surplus Food

Provider staff logs leftover food at the end of each day — just pick the food type and enter the quantity.

🔔
2

Receivers Get Notified

Our system instantly finds nearby Receivers within 10km and sends them real-time alerts about available food.

🚚
3

Claim & Collect

Receivers claim the food, get map directions to the provider, and pick it up — ensuring zero food goes to waste.

Smart Features

Powered by AI and real-time data to maximize food redistribution.

🤖

AI Waste Forecasting

ML model predicts next week's food waste by type, helping providers plan better and reduce surplus.

📍

Geolocation Matching

Haversine-based proximity matching automatically connects providers with the nearest receivers.

📊

Visual Analytics

Interactive charts showing waste trends, forecast accuracy, and redistribution impact over time.

🗺️

Live Map & Routing

Google Maps integration with real-time routing from receiver to provider for efficient pickups.

⚡

Instant Notifications

Email alerts sent immediately when surplus food is logged, so receivers can act fast.

📱

Simple Interface

Dead simple UI for provider staff — just a dropdown and a number. No training needed.
